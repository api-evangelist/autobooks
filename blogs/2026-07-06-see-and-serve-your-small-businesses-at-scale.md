Key takeaways: A financial institution has never had a tool built to manage its small business portfolio. Bankers stretch general-purpose CRMs and pull static reports out of digital banking, neither designed for small business. Autobooks is one connected system with two sides: the tools a small business uses to run its business, and the reporting and relationship tools the institution uses to manage the whole portfolio.
